# Brief 03 — Four-Hat Identity (one operator, four stations)

**Route:** `/` section (below hero) · **Hats:** all four, shown together · **Phase:** P1
Implements `design/design-story.md §3` (metaphor: *one operator, four stations*). This is the
*identity structure* — reconceived from zero, **NOT** the scrapped "Orchestration" visual.

**Mood:** engineered authority. One person who genuinely holds four disciplines at once — shown as a
single instrument with four accent-coded readouts, not four separate people or four cards.

**Composition & Hierarchy:** a single wide **instrument** presenting all four hats simultaneously —
**Engineer · Auditor · PM · Founder** — each a station with its accent, a one-line claim, and its
strongest **verified proof** (a `<Claim>` from brief 02):
- **Engineer (cyan):** ships the AI end-to-end — `Overmind · 13-phase · 51 modules`, `192,000+
  corpus`, `1,345 tests · 100% coverage`. Links to the systems surface.
- **Auditor (violet):** AI-driven security — `CodeHawks #124 · 17 findings (8 High) · 1,430 EXP`
  (public deep-link) + `KTHULHU · reproduces every finding as an executable exploit`.
- **PM (green):** the delivery substrate — `20+ years · 7 countries · 20+ industrial plants ·
  AgilePM® Practitioner` (FR-060, the seniority anchor — a *separate* anchor, not a flagship slot).
- **Founder (warm gold):** live products, paying users — AgileGypsy Labs · KTHULHU · Kointel.
A **filter control** lets the visitor emphasize one hat: selecting it **dims the other three to a
low-contrast state — never hides them** (BR-07). The default state shows all four at full presence.

**Key Moment:** selecting a hat and watching the other three **dim in place** (still legible, still
there) — the design literally refusing to hide any dimension of the person. This is the four-hat law
made physical.

**Palette Accents:** the four per-hat accents (cyan/violet/green/warm-gold) color-code each station —
nodes, edge, the accent rule on the active station. Dimming reduces contrast/opacity, never removes.
Cyan remains the global lead (Engineer + AI-as-headline).

**Animation Strategy:** filter = a ≤200ms contrast/opacity transition on the non-selected stations
(dim, not collapse); one entrance rise for the section (≤400ms, once, staggered ≤80ms per station).
No looping.

**Spatial Layout:** a single horizontal instrument on desktop (four stations across, joined by a
thin **validated-edge** spine that reads as "one system, four outputs" — the node+edge motif at
identity scale); stacks to four full-width stations on mobile, filter as a segmented control on top.
**Not** a four-column card grid (rejected #6) — the stations share one continuous instrument frame,
they are not four detached cards.

**3D Elements:** none. **Glass Effects:** none (flat hairline instrument, corner-ticks).

**Typography:** hat names 13–14px uppercase mono labels; the one-line claims in engineered grotesk
~18px; all proof figures mono tabular; sentence case for prose.

**References:** the studio's "one mind, many arms" *concept* (carry the idea, not the octopus);
Linear feature-row pacing; aircraft annunciator label grammar for the station readouts.

**Key Constraint:** all four hats are **visible together on one surface**, and a filter **DIMS,
never fully hides** (FR-003, BR-07). The section must read as *one person*, not four.

**HARD survival constraints:**
1. **Not** a skills/tech-icon grid, **not** proficiency bars (rejected #2) — proficiency is shown as
   verified proof per hat, never as a bar or a logo cloud.
2. **Not** the old four-column pattern or stacked cards (rejected #6) — one continuous instrument.
3. Filters **DIM, never hide** — hiding a hat violates the four-hat law (BR-07); no tab that shows
   one hat and removes the rest.
4. The PM delivery record is the **seniority anchor**, presented as instrumented credentials — **not**
   promoted into the four flagship slots (FR-060), and **not** a chronological résumé timeline as a
   primary section (avoid-list).
5. Every per-hat number is a cleared `<Claim>` with a receipt; forbidden claims (PMP, PRINCE2-
   Practitioner) never appear on the PM station.
6. Founder accent stays a distinct **warm gold** confined to this hat — never a site-wide orange (C7;
   pending John's confirm, story §10 Q1).
