# 04 — Search Intent & Arrival (how the personas reach jw3b.dev)

**Reality check first:** a senior personal portfolio is **not primarily an SEO play.** The
dominant arrival paths are *referral, direct-name lookup, GitHub, X/LinkedIn, and job-board /
audit-platform profiles* — not organic search for a generic head term (John will not out-rank
"hire AI developer" against agencies, nor should he try). SEO's real job here is to **not
embarrass** on a branded search and to be *findable on the specific, low-competition niche
terms* where his positioning is uncontested. Keyword table below is intent-classified;
**volumes are `[UNVERIFIED — no keyword-tool access this session; requires manual validation]`**
— per the no-fabrication rule, I give *qualitative* demand signal from grounded sources, not
invented numbers.

## Arrival channels, ranked (where design/SEO effort should go)

1. **Branded / direct** — "John Wellard", "jw3b", "jw3b.dev", "AgileGypsy". *Must win
   flawlessly.* Someone was referred and is checking him out — this is the highest-intent,
   highest-conversion moment. `[INFERRED — standard for personal brand]`
2. **GitHub → site** — commit cadence + real repos are a screening signal ("consistent commit
   history reads as professional discipline"). Design implication: GitHub and the live site
   must reinforce each other. `[GROUNDED: Fonzi]`
3. **Public audit record → site** — CodeHawks/Code4rena profile is a verifiable arrival path
   for Secondary A; buyers start from the leaderboard. `[GROUNDED: Blockonomi/Sherlock]`
4. **Social proof (X/LinkedIn threads)** — "proof, not promises" unedited-run posts are the
   natural top-of-funnel for the Primary persona. `[INFERRED]`
5. **Niche organic search** — the long-tail terms below, where competition is low.

## The content-gap play (highest ROI)

The **informational** terms above ("zero-trust validator", "systems are graphs",
"agentic reliability / eval infra") are **high-intent for the Primary persona and have almost
no authoritative answers** — this is the classic "high demand, weak supply" gap. John can own
them *with the exact live artifacts the site already contains* (the validated-pipeline visual,
the graph, the unedited runs). SEO and differentiation collapse into the same asset: the proof
surfaces double as the ranking content.
