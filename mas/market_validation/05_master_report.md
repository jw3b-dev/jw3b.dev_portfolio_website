# 05 — Master Validation Report — jw3b.dev rebuild

**Role:** market-researcher (MAS Phase 0) · **Date:** 2026-08-15
**Subject:** ground-up rebuild of jw3b.dev — John Wellard, "Senior Agentic AI Developer."
**Prior design:** REJECTED by owner as generic ("the same scrap" — two-column hero + stacked
cards + cyber/neon glass). This report arms the design phase to build the opposite.

---

## Verdict: ✅ PROCEED (with one hard constraint)

**Confidence: HIGH** on positioning & anti-generic direction; **MEDIUM** on absolute search
demand (no keyword-tool data). Proceed to design **conditioned on** the live-reliability
constraint (below) and reconciliation of John's stat claims with the evidence register.

**Primary persona:** the **Building Founder/CTO (seed–Series A)** who needs an *agentic-AI
system that survives production, not a demo.* This segment lands squarely on John's rarest,
least-contested asset (live multi-agent systems + a zero-trust validator + real test rigor)
and directly answers its #1 fear ("works in demos, fails in production"). Secondary: Web3
audit buyers (served by the verifiable CodeHawks record + live KTHULHU) and senior-AI hiring
managers (served by "three real systems + failures shown"). All three are satisfied by the
*same* proof-first architecture — no conflict, only emphasis.

---

## Why proceed (the evidence, compressed)

1. **The market gap is real and John sits in it.** "Agentic AI orchestration" + "responsible-AI
   documentation" are called out as **the most underrepresented, high-signal, low-competition**
   skills in candidate portfolios. John's entire practice is exactly this. (Fonzi/DataExpert.)
2. **Buyers already buy the way John can prove.** Founders/CTOs screen for *evaluation
   infrastructure and production reliability*, not credentials (Islands HQ, Khosla). Audit
   buyers buy on *externally verifiable leaderboard evidence*, not logo walls
   (Blockonomi/Sherlock/TalentLevelUp). John has both the reliability engineering and the
   public record.
3. **The generic field is beatable on a new axis.** At the award tier, **3D/motion is now
   baseline, not a differentiator** (Muzli 2026). Competitors are stuck competing on *looks*.
   John can move the contest to **live proof**, where the field is nearly empty because almost
   no one has running systems, a public record, and the nerve to show failures.

## Competitive landscape (one-paragraph synthesis)

Three registers matter. **(a) Award-caliber craft** (Bruno Simon, Obys, Rauno) sets the
*interaction/typography bar* — but its content is brand vibes and its 3D is now table stakes.
**(b) Premium-technical / AI-content** (Linear, Maxime Heckel's live inline demos, Anthropic's
evidence-density) is the register John should actually own — proof and precision over
spectacle. **(c) The generic baseline** — glassmorphism-neon dev templates (the rejected
current look) and one-click terminal-CLI templates — is exactly "the same scrap" and must be
escaped, not iterated. John's edge over *all three* is that his portfolio can be **operable
and verifiable**, which none of the generic tier and few of the award tier can claim.

## Pain points / market gap (what decision-makers complain about)

- "Leading with skills lists and burying the actual projects." (top portfolio mistake — Fonzi)
- "A clean notebook doesn't tell you about production readiness." (CTOs — Islands HQ)
- "Works in demos, fails in production." (the founding-engineer failure mode — Islands HQ)
- "The firm's brand matters far less than the specific researchers… you get whoever is
  available." (audit buyers want a *named*, verifiable individual — Blockonomi)
- Portfolios rarely show *what failed and why*, though managers explicitly screen for it. (Fonzi)

**The gap:** decision-makers want **operable, verifiable, honest proof of production-grade
systems** — and almost no portfolio gives it. That gap is jw3b.dev's to take.

---

## ★ Distinctive-direction brief (the design phase MUST honor this)

> **Build a portfolio you operate, not one you read.** jw3b.dev's differentiation is not its
> rendering — it is that John's systems are *live, verifiable, and honestly unedited*, and the
> site must BE those systems rather than describe them. **AVOID** every marker of "the same
> scrap": the headshot-and-tagline two-column hero, the skills/tech-icon grid, the uniform
> stack of feature cards, the résumé timeline, and — critically — the reflex to reach for a
> glassmorphism-neon skin or a decorative 3D/WebGL hero, both of which are now the *generic
> default* and would re-file John under "creative-dev template," the opposite of a senior
> systems builder. **LEAN INTO** three moves competitors structurally cannot copy: (1) a hero
> that is an *operable proof surface* — run an audit, query the agent, explore the graph — so
> the first impression is "wait, this is actually working," not "nice animation"; (2)
> **verification as the visual signature** — the "systems are graphs" thesis and the zero-trust
> validated pipeline rendered as an explorable, steppable object, because that visual IS the
> reliability the primary buyer is purchasing; (3) **radical honesty** — a first-class
> "unedited run, failures included" surface, the exact trait hiring managers hunt for and
> almost no portfolio dares show. Aesthetic register: premium-technical (Linear-precise,
> Rauno-dense, Anthropic-evidence-forward), dark and fast and confident — density with a spine,
> where a single unmistakable "hire me" path survives the spectacle.

## Risks (carry into architecture/QA)

1. **Live-reliability = existential (HARD CONSTRAINT).** A live surface that fails on load
   *disproves* the whole thesis. Every operable surface needs a graceful cached/replay
   fallback so "live" degrades to "verifiably real," never "broken." **Non-negotiable.**
2. **Claims vs evidence register.** GraphRAG +18pts, 192K corpus, 1,345 tests/100% cov,
   "paying users," Overmind 13-phase — all **client-supplied; reconcile with
   `docs/PORTFOLIO_REFERENCE.md` before any appears as fact** (project claims-discipline rule).
   `[UNVERIFIED]`
3. **Spectacle burying conversion.** Proof-heavy sites can hide the "hire me" action — protect
   a single spine path.
4. **SA-location / timezone friction** for remote hire — mitigate by foregrounding async proof
   over "available for standups." `[INFERRED]`
5. **Performance/complexity** — live embedded systems threaten load speed and mobile; frame
   budgets and fallbacks are a design input, not an afterthought.

## Confidence & Limitations

- **HIGH confidence** (3+ independent sources across 2 tiers — hiring playbooks + portfolio
  guides + design-media) on: primary-persona choice, the clichés-to-avoid catalogue, the
  distinctive-moves catalogue, and the "3D is now baseline" reframing.
- **MEDIUM** on: absolute search-demand volumes (no keyword-tool access — all volumes
  `[UNVERIFIED]`; channel *priority* is sound but sizing is not), and the current live state of
  `[KNOWN]` reference sites (rauno.me, Linear, Heckel, Anthropic not re-fetched this session —
  spot-check before locking any as a design reference).
- **Dissenting evidence retained (not omitted):** sources do **not** call glassmorphism or
  terminal portfolios "overdone" — they're described as *sustained, popular* 2026 choices.
  My "avoid" ruling is *specific to a distinctive senior rebuild*, where ubiquity = generic;
  it is not a claim that these styles are universally disliked. Stated plainly so the design
  phase weighs it.
- **Commercially-incentivised sources flagged:** recruiting/audit vendors (Recruiting-from-
  Scratch, Islands HQ, Sherlock, TalentLevelUp) and design-media (Muzli/AWWWARDS) each have
  slant; every load-bearing claim is corroborated by an independent outlet or by the outlet's
  own disconfirming admission.
- **Not done (scope-limited on purpose):** no hands-on trial signup/crawl of each reference
  (they are references, not SaaS competitors); no primary-source interviews; no analytics on
  the old site's traffic.
